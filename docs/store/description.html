Catch them, or shoot back.

The Hunted is a goofy chase-and-catch couch platformer, not a horror game. Every round one player is the vampire and the other is the peasant, picked at random. The vampire is faster and wins by catching the peasant. The peasant is slower but can fire bullets. Play against the built-in AI or share one keyboard with a friend.

How to play

  • VS AI: you play both keysets against a computer opponent that has its own vampire and peasant tactics.
  • PVP: two players on one keyboard.
  • Roles are random each round, so the hunter becomes the hunted and back again.
  • Only the peasant fires, so run, jump and aim; the vampire just has to touch you.

Controls

  • Player 1: arrow keys to move and jump, Right Ctrl to fire.
  • Player 2: W, A and D to move and jump, Space to fire.
  • VS AI: both keysets move your character.
  • Menus: mouse. Esc goes back one page. Fullscreen: F11.

Three levels

  • Classic: two wide ledges and a high platform between them.
  • Towers: stacked stone platforms with a high perch to fight over.
  • Arena: a cathedral hall with seven platforms to jump between.

Play it

Play in your browser right here (desktop browser with a keyboard; click the game once to start, and use the fullscreen button for a bigger picture), or download a build.

  • Windows: run the installer hunted-windows-setup.exe, or unzip and run hunted.exe. The game isn't code-signed, so the first launch may show "Windows protected your PC": click More info, then Run anyway.
  • macOS: open Hunted.app. The app isn't signed, so right-click it and choose Open the first time.
  • Linux: run ./hunted/hunted.

About this game

This is my first pygame project, kept as a learning artifact and polished up since. The code is open source (MIT) and built with Python and pygame-ce. The code, devlog and changelog are on GitHub; bug reports and ideas are welcome in its issues.

AI disclosure: the art is AI-generated with OpenAI Codex image generation and cleaned up to one shared pixel-art palette. Claude Code was a coding assistant. The music and sounds are CC0 packs, not AI. Game design, art direction, playtesting and review are mine.
